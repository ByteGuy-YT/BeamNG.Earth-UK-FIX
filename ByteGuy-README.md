-More accurate UK road widths, lane counts and layouts

-Reworked motorways, junctions, slip roads and roundabouts

-Improved motorway gantries, road signs and speed limit signs

-Added and improved traffic lights and speed cameras

-Better bridge generation and handling of overlapping and underground roads

-Improved buildings, shops, bus stops and car parks

-Improved grass, terrain, water and general land generation

-Numerous bug fixes, visual improvements and fixes for road generation glitches

-Fixed sidewalls and added street lamps

-Reworked road generation and bridge generation as well as wall generation so alot less glitchy globally

-Roads can now overlap and large complex road bridges can generate correctly with correct number of lanes (see Boston USA)
